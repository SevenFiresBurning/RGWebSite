Music & Media.

Artist production, collaboration, publishing and artist support.

Sound, image, performance.

A moving sample of the work: collaboration, recording, publishing, and the room around it.

Music & Media at Resistance & Ground brings together artist production, collaboration, publishing, media, promotion, and artist support under one roof.

Read more

R&G works with artists not only on the business around the music, but on the music itself — producing projects, helping shape arrangements and recordings, and fostering collaborations between artists, musicians, and other creatives.

The focus is practical: make strong work, protect creative rights, understand the business, and build the infrastructure needed to release and promote that work effectively.

Services can include artist production, recording support, collaborative project development, publishing administration, rights and royalty education, release planning, media creation, promotional support, artist websites, and other project-specific development.

Publishing remains central to that work, with an emphasis on transparency, education, and creative ownership.

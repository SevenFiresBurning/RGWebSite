YesChef

Restaurant-focused costing and operations tool based on real kitchen workflows.

Digital Product / Business System

In Development

YesChef is in development, informed by real kitchen workflows.

Restaurant operations, built around the way kitchens actually work.

Running a kitchen means juggling recipes, food costs, inventory, vendors, prep lists, and events. Most of that information lives in separate spreadsheets, invoices, notebooks, or somebody's head. YesChef is being developed to bring those moving parts together.

Built with working kitchens in mind, YesChef connects ingredients, vendor products, recipes, and pricing into a practical system. The goal is to help chefs understand what they're spending, what they're making, and where the numbers stop adding up.

What YesChef is built to do

  • Recipe costing: Calculate ingredient costs, portion costs, food-cost percentages, and suggested selling prices.
  • Recipe management: Organize recipes, adjust quantities, and scale production without rebuilding calculations.
  • Vendor comparisons: Compare ingredient pricing across suppliers and make more informed purchasing decisions.
  • Inventory and ordering: Connect ingredients to vendor products, pack sizes, and purchasing needs.
  • Events and catering: Plan production quantities, calculate requirements, and generate organized prep and order lists.
  • Operational reporting: Bring revenue and food costs together for a clearer picture of kitchen performance.

Development

YesChef is an active R&G development project. The current work focuses on reliable costing calculations, connected ingredient and recipe data, practical event planning, and a straightforward interface.

Future development includes invoice importing, deeper inventory integration, and improved reporting.

The objective isn't to bury chefs in another management platform. It's to give them useful information, reduce repetitive work, and leave more time for cooking.

Status: In development.

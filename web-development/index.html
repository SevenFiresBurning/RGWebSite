Web Development.

Web Development at Resistance & Ground focuses on websites, digital products, and practical systems built around real problems.

That includes client websites, workflow tools, business systems, and original interactive projects. Current development includes YesChef, a restaurant-focused operations and costing tool, and AfterFall, a strategy game built around resources, territory, diplomacy, infrastructure, and consequence.

The common thread is simple: understand the problem, reduce friction, and build something useful.

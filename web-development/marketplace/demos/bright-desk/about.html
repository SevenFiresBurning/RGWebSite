About

Hi, I’m Emma.

I teach upper elementary, build practical resources, and care a little too much about making directions clearer.

I started The Bright Desk because the best classroom tools are usually the ones that make a hard part of teaching feel simpler—not louder, cuter, or more complicated.

My resources focus on reading, writing, routines, and the small systems that save teachers time while keeping students thinking.
